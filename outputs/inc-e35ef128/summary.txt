Overall severity is P1 with major user impact: payment requests are failing, the payment deployment is down to `1/6` available replicas, and authentication is intermittently blocking profile access. The single most critical next step is to immediately pause or roll back the `payment-service` rollout and shed `POST /api/v1/payments` traffic to stop the cascading DB, OOM, and gateway failures.
