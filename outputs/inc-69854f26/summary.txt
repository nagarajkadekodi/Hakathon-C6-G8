The incident is a P1 production outage cluster centered on `payment-service`, with `DB_CONN_TIMEOUT` errors, PostgreSQL connection pool exhaustion on `postgres-primary` (`remaining connection slots are reserved...`), `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: Java heap space` followed by `OOMKilled`/`CrashLoopBackOff`, and downstream 502/504s from `api-gateway` on `POST /api/v1/payments`.  
`auth-service` and `redis-cache` are also affected by `TOKEN_VALIDATION_FAILED` / Redis session lookup timeouts causing 401s, while `kubernetes` and the `deployment-controller` report `payment-service` availability down to `1/6` replicas after rollout `v2.8.4`.  
Overall severity is P1 with immediate user impact on checkout/payment completion and some login/session validation failures; users are seeing timeouts, gateway errors, and failed transactions in the production path.  
The single most critical next step is to roll back `payment-service` from `v2.8.4` to `v2.8.3` and stabilize it, while reducing load on `postgres-primary` to stop the cascade of DB timeouts and crash loops.
